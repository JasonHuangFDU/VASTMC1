1 - Design and develop visualizations and visual analytic tools that will allow Silas to explore and understand the profile of Sailor Shift锟斤拷s career.

a. Who has she been most influenced by over time?

b. Who has she collaborated with and directly or indirectly influenced?

c. How has she influenced collaborators of the broader Oceanus Folk community?

 

 

Figure 1-1: View a is force-directed graph. Legend a1 is the Node & Edge Legend. It displays the visual style for different node types (Song, Person, MusicalGroup, Album, RecordLabel) in the graph. "Sailor Shift" is treated as a special node with a distinct color, which is also shown in the legend. A "Notable Node" signifies that the node is considered prominent. For Song and Album nodes, this "Notable" status is provided directly in the dataset. For Person, MusicalGroup, and RecordLabel nodes, the "Notable" attribute is computationally derived to indicate whether the entity is famous.

The edge types are divided into four categories:

Creation ('PerformerOf', 'ComposerOf', 'ProducerOf', 'LyricistOf'): Refers to a Person or MusicalGroup creating a musical work.

Inspiration ('InStyleOf', 'CoverOf', 'DirectlySamples', 'InterpolatesFrom', 'LyricalReferenceTo'): Indicates the sources of inspiration for a musical work.

Commercial ('RecordedBy', 'DistributedBy'): Signifies the involvement of a music company in releasing a musical work, indicating a commercial relationship.

Membership ('MemberOf'): Denotes that a Person belongs to a MusicalGroup.

Legend a2 is the Genre Legend. It displays the color-coding used in the force-directed graph to distinguish between the different genres of the music.

Control panel a3 at the top is the Hop Count Control. It sets the hop limit for the force-directed graph. For example, selecting "One Hop" will render the center node, its immediate neighbors (nodes one hop away), and the corresponding edges.

Control button a4 allows the user to toggle the force-directed graph's display between Sailor's collaborative relationships and influence relationships.

View b is a bubble chart that visually represents the individuals who influenced Sailor Shift and the magnitude of their influence.

2 - Develop visualizations that illustrate how the influence of Oceanus Folk has spread through the musical world. 

a. Was this influence intermittent or did it have a gradual rise?

b. What genres and top artists have been most influenced by Oceanus Folk?

c. On the converse, how has Oceanus Folk changed with the rise of Sailor Shift? From which genres does it draw most of its contemporary inspiration?

 

a. Was this influence intermittent or did it have a gradual rise?

 

Figure 2-1: This is a combination of a line chart and a stacked column chart, showing how the influence of Oceanus Folk has changed over time. The influence value is measured by the number of times works from the Oceanus Folk genre have been referred to by works from other genres.

 

The influence of Oceanus Folk exhibits a clearly intermittent rise, rather than a gradual one (Figure 2-1). Its influence shows cyclical bursts approximately every 6 to 7 years, with peak years occurring in 1991, 1997, 2004, and 2010. After 2010, the cycle shortened to 3-4 years, with the influence value reaching a peak of 13 in 2013, followed by a decline between 2017 and 2020. In 2023, the influence reached its all-time high of 17, a development that is likely closely related to Sailor Shift joining Ivy Echoes.

 

 

Figure 2-2: Click 'Genre Comparison' to enter comparison mode. Hover over or click a genre's legend/segment to highlight it; a tooltip will show its annual influence for comparison.

 

Viewed on a per-genre basis, the influence of Oceanus is not a gradual rise either, but is instead characterized by peaks (Figure 2-2).

 

b. What genres and top artists have been most influenced by Oceanus Folk?

 

 

Figure 2-3: This Sankey diagram provides two analysis modes: Direction Comparison shows the influence of and influence on Oceanus Folk; Temporal Comparison shows the changes in Oceanus Folk's sources of inspiration before and after the rise of Sailor Shift in 2028. The flow direction in the diagram is Oceanus Folk→Genre→Artist, where the width of the flows represents the strength of influence, measured by the number of creative edges or inspiration edges. The slider at the top allows adjustment of the number of displayed genres (default 6) and artists (default 10).

 

Genre Level: The genre most influenced by Oceanus Folk is Desert Rock, with an influence value of 50. Following closely are Indie Folk and Dream Pop, both with an influence value of 42, while Space Rock ranks fourth with an influence value of 35 (Figure 2-4).

 

 

 

Figure 2-4: When hovering the mouse over the flow between Oceanus Folk and the genres, a summary of the influencing edges will be displayed. The genre most influenced by Oceanus Folk is Desert Rock, with an influence value of 50. However, the greatest source of inspiration for Oceanus Folk comes from the Indie Folk genre.

 

Artist Level: The distribution of influence at the artist level is very dense and relatively uniform, indicating that Oceanus Folk's impact is widespread and dispersed, reaching numerous artists across multiple genres. Keith Larsen and Xia Zhu are two of the more prominently influenced artists (Figure 2-5).

 

 

Figure 2-5: When hovering the mouse over the flow between genres and artists, a summary of the collaborative edges will be displayed. Keith Larsen and Xia Zhu are the two artists most notably influenced by Oceanus Folk.

 

c. On the converse, how has Oceanus Folk changed with the rise of Sailor Shift? From which genres does it draw most of its contemporary inspiration?

 

Figure 2-6: Oceanus Folk's sources of inspiration: before and after the rise of Sailor Shift in 2028

 

The rise of Sailor Shift marks a key turning point in the evolution of Oceanus Folk's musical style (Figure 2-5). Before 2028, the sources of inspiration for Oceanus Folk's work were relatively concentrated, with Indie Folk's influence value reaching as high as 83. After 2028, however, it no longer relied primarily on a single genre, but instead drew from more balanced and diverse sources of inspiration, with genres like Desert Rock and Indie Rock joining the ranks of its main influences.

a. Visualize the careers of three artists. Compare and contrast their rise in popularity and influence.

 

To visualize the career trajectories of three artists, a composite visualization was developed to illustrate their respective rises in popularity and influence.

 

Figure 3-1: The line chart illustrates the growing influence of three artists over time, reflecting metrics such as their notable productions, collaboration frequency, and their impact on other artists-including stylistic borrowings, adaptations, covers, tributes, and resampling. The bar chart depicts the annual collaboration frequency of three artists with others. The data points on the line chart represent the number of new works released each year, with blue highlights indicating the years in which hit works were released. The donut chart displays the genre distribution of the artists’ works. When the cursor hovers over different areas of the chart, the career trajectory chart dynamically presents detailed information on influence, number of new works, collaboration frequency, and genre composition for the corresponding year.

 

Figure 3-1 shows the career trajectories of Sailor Shift and two notable artists she has collaborated with, Beatrice Albright and Daniel O'Connell and their collaborative relationships and personal influences are illustrated in Figure 3-2 and Figure 3-3. In 2028, a single by Sailor Shift suddenly skyrocketed to fame, topping global charts. Since her rise to prominence, she has released a high volume of quality work nearly every year while experimenting across diverse genres. While actively promoting Oceanus Folk and helping unknown artists enter the music scene, she maintains extensive collaborations. Correspondingly, Beatrice Albright and Daniel O'Connell debuted in 2031 with Sailor Shift's collaborative support, each releasing substantial works that year and gaining notable influence. Later, they collaborated again in 2034 and 2035, establishing recognition within the Oceanus genre.

1. Given the task to develop visualizations for knowledge graphs, did you find that the challenge pushed you to develop new techniques for visual representation?

 

Although we did not adopt innovative visualization methods, we selected the appropriate traditional visualizations for each question. For example, we used  force-directed graphs, sankey diagrams,, and other charts that were very suitable for analyzing the task. Our system has good interactivity, allowing users to easily explore the issues. 3. What was the most difficult part of working on this year’s data and what could have made it more accessible?

 

One of the biggest challenges in this task was the large volume of data, which caused slow graph loading speeds and impacted overall response time. During data exploration, we also encountered some data quality issues. For example, during the data preprocessing phase, we found that the source and target nodes of the "RecordedBy" edge were swapped; additionally, some data did not match the information in the data description, such as the relationship between certain musical groups and Sailor Shift being incorrectly labeled as "InStyleOf." These issues led to inconsistencies in the data as we explored influence and collaboration relationships, increasing the workload for data cleaning.
